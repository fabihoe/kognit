Support — Goal Seek for Google Sheets

Questions, bug reports and feature requests.

Contact: kontakt@arbeitstyp.de
Response time: we usually reply within two business days (Monday–Friday, Central European Time).

Common questions

  • "No sign change found" means the add-on tested a range of values and your formula never crossed the target. Check whether a solution exists; a different starting value or bounds (under Advanced) often help.
  • "The formula only jumps in steps" appears for staircase formulas (SUMIFS, FLOOR, lookup buckets): no input hits the target exactly, so the closest achievable value is offered instead.
  • A banner about an earlier run means a run was interrupted (closed sidebar, lost connection). One click restores the original cell content — nothing is lost.
  • Daily limit: the free plan includes 5 runs per day, resetting at midnight Central European Time.

What to include in a bug report

  • What you did and what you expected to happen
  • The exact message shown in the sidebar (result card or error text)
  • The target formula's rough shape (e.g. "SUMIFS over a date range") — not your data
  • Your spreadsheet locale (File → Settings), if the issue involves number input
  • No spreadsheets, credentials or personal data, please. We never need access to your file — and the add-on itself sends us nothing.

Feature requests

Ideas are welcome at the same address. Short, concrete descriptions of what you were trying to do work best.
