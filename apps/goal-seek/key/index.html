How to activate

  1. Open your spreadsheet → Extensions → Goal Seek → Open Goal Seek.
  2. At the bottom of the sidebar, open Goal Seek Pro.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate.

The key works on the Google account you use with the add-on. Keep it somewhere safe — and if anything goes wrong, we can always re-send it: kontakt@arbeitstyp.de.
