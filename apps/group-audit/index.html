Group Audit for Jira

See every place a group is used in Jira — before you change or delete it.

Read-only Runs on Atlassian CSV export

Jira can't tell you where a group is used (JRACLOUD-71967, 1,000+ votes, open since 2019). So admins delete groups blind, break permissions, and rebuild access by support ticket. Group Audit answers the question in seconds.

What it scans

  • Permission schemes — with the projects that use them
  • Notification schemes, incl. group-custom-field notifications flagged for manual review
  • Project roles in every project, plus default role actors
  • Issue security scheme levels
  • Filter and dashboard sharing & editing grants
  • Product access (application role groups)
  • Member counts — including deactivated accounts still in the group
Honest by design. If any check cannot finish, the report is marked incomplete — in the UI and in the CSV export — because an audit tool must never silently omit. What the app can't scan (workflow conditions, global permission grants) is disclosed openly instead of hidden.

Privacy in one sentence

The app is read-only, stores nothing, and no data ever leaves your Atlassian instance — see the privacy policy.
