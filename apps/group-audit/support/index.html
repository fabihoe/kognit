Support — Group Audit for Jira

Questions, bug reports and feature requests.

Contact: kontakt@arbeitstyp.de
Response time: we usually reply within two business days (Monday–Friday, Central European Time).

Before you write

  • The documentation covers setup, the CSV column reference and the most common issues (incomplete scans, license banner, slow scans on large sites).
  • An “Incomplete scan” banner is usually transient Jira rate limiting — running the scan again typically resolves it.

What to include in a bug report

  • What you did and what you expected to happen
  • The step that failed (from the progress checklist) and the exact error text — the “Scan error” rows of the CSV export are perfect
  • Rough site size (number of projects/filters), if the issue involves a large scan
  • A screenshot, if the issue is visual
  • No credentials, tokens or personal data, please. We never need access to your Jira instance — and the app itself sends us nothing.

Feature requests

Very welcome — the roadmap is driven by admin needs (workflow-condition scanning is the current front-runner). One sentence about your use case helps us prioritise.

Security

Suspected security issue? Please email kontakt@arbeitstyp.de with “SECURITY” in the subject line — such reports are read first.
